help_request: asks for help setting up, building, learning, fixing, or deciding something
for the writer's own project or channel (content creation, task automation,
or something physical like a shelf for books, figures/plushies, or game cases/carts).
analytical_inquiry: Brings specific, checkable evidence the writer has gathered (a date, a stat, 
a quote, a named comparison, a cited work) and uses it to ask the community an open question about 
the world: whether something is true, whether something changed, whether an observed pattern is real, 
or why. 
reaction: A response to a specific thing that happened to the writer or that they witnessed 
(an event, result, milestone, change, or piece of news), whether it just happened or not.
general_discussion: a post that doesn't fit `reaction`, `help_request`, or `analytical_inquiry`.
Typically opens a conversation, asks for opinions, experiences, or
recommendations for general interest, or shares news or updates.
